## Overview of BEX Construction Services

BEX Construction Services, founded in 2014, is a design-build general contractor headquartered in Maryland Heights, Missouri. The company primarily serves the St. Louis region and operates in various sectors, including commercial, educational/institutional, healthcare, industrial/process, restaurant, retail, and warehouse markets. BEX Construction Services has gained recognition for its rapid growth and commitment to community engagement, making it a prominent player in the Midwestern U.S. construction industry ([BEX Construction About](https://bexstl.com/about/)).

The company employs between 11 and 50 professionals and has been recognized multiple times for its excellence in construction services. Notably, BEX has been awarded "General Contractor of the Year" by the American Subcontractors Association Midwest Council three times (2019, 2020, and 2022) ([MHCC News](https://www.mhcc.com/news/2024/11/grace-bueckendorf-wright-joins-bex-construction-services-as-vice-president/)).
